## Fixed alternatives, rules and ties

For each eligible model, weather casew and feasible actiona, compute normalized simulated site-energy gain g(a, w)=100*(E_baseline(w)-E_action(w))/E_baseline(w), in percentage points of that case's baseline. The no-action alternative0has gain0and is always feasible. Negative gains remain negative. Electricity-energy differences are reported separately in kWh and normalized units; they are not treated as money, carbon or additive package effects.

Three prespecified rules share the identical feasible set: nominal selects the largest2018gain; maximin selects the largest minimum gain across the two cases; minimax regret selects the smallest maximum regret across the two cases, where regret(a, w)=max_b g(b, w)-g(a, w). Exact objective ties prefer the lower numeric upgrade ID, with no action 0 first. Export full tied-top sets and distinguish strict unique-top reversals from changes involving a tie. Export every action's gain, regret, dominance/rank and each rule's selected action, case outcomes, worst gain, worst regret and equal-case descriptive average. Equal-case averages are not probabilities for future weather.

The maximin/minimax inequalities within this assumed set are mathematical properties, not empirical superiority or novelty. The substantive observations are the actual choice disagreements, ranking reversals, regret magnitudes, negative gains, nonapplicability and source failures in these specific published simulations. Do not call the selected choice globally optimal, robust to all future weather, cost-effective or validated for investment. No retrofit cost, energy tariff, carbon factor, dispatch or field measurement is supplied here.
